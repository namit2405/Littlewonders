Welcome to Little Wonders

A warm, nurturing place for your child to explore, grow and shine

Little Wonders ELC is a warm, nurturing early learning centre where every child is encouraged to explore, grow and shine with confidence. Our thoughtfully designed, play based programs build strong foundations in learning, creativity and social development.

Our flagship centre is the culmination of over 25 years of experience, an exciting education and wellbeing model that brings the best ideas, philosophies and experience together in one architecturally designed space.
